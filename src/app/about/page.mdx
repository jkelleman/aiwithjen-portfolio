## The Core Thesis

Most enterprise AI conversations get stuck in an unhelpful binary: either treating language as a decorative conversational surface (chatbots, generic prompts, autocomplete) or treating engineering as pure backend plumbing (vector databases, compute clusters, batch jobs).

**I build in the space between.**

As a Content Design Engineer and AI Strategist, my work treats language as formal system infrastructure. In complex, distributed data platforms, language isn't just UI copy—it is the semantic layer, the schema validation contract, the intent router, and the boundary condition for autonomous execution. When models interact with mission-critical lakehouses, words dictate state transitions, define error recovery loops, and mathematically govern whether a pipeline commits cleanly or corrupts downstream tables.

## Systems Architecture Meets Language Design

My path to AI systems design wasn't traditional. Coming from small-town Kentucky with a self-taught technical foundation, I learned technology by dissecting how systems fail at their boundaries. That background shaped how I approach complex enterprise problems: with a builder’s pragmatic curiosity and an architectural demand for clarity.

Leading user experience strategy for artificial intelligence applications across big data ecosystems taught me that the hardest challenges in AI aren't model size—they're ergonomics, state management, and semantic alignment.

- **In Lakehouse Data Engineering:** Translating distributed OneLake and Spark operations into persistent, self-healing agentic workflows (like [Project Osmos](/work/project-osmos)) requires moving beyond ephemeral chat prompts. It demands deterministic pre-flight contracts, runtime AST validation, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints that respect enterprise compute budgets.
- **In Semantic Architecture:** Large datasets are unusable without shared ontologies. I design the taxonomies, metadata governance models, and naming grammars that eliminate ambiguous column references—enabling both human analysts and autonomous agents to query data with mathematical precision.
- **In Agentic Ergonomics:** Autonomous agents fail when their state is opaque. I design cross-surface interaction patterns (connecting local terminal tools like GitHub Copilot CLI and Claude Code directly to cloud workspaces) that ensure long-running batch tasks survive disconnections, recover from schema drift, and deliver auditable code artifacts.

## Expanding the AI Workforce: Advocacy in Practice

Technological revolutions often concentrate opportunity among those who already hold the keys. I believe the future of autonomous systems and applied AI depends on broadening who gets to design, interrogate, and steer these models.

That conviction drives my work outside the codebase:

- **Massachusetts AI Future Workforce Initiative:** As an active proponent, I work to connect non-traditional learners, career switchers, and creative practitioners to high-impact technical pathways in artificial intelligence.
- **Demystifying PromptOps & Systems Design:** I mentor emerging designers and technologists on how to transition from traditional visual UX into content design engineering, prompt evaluation harnesses, and deterministic state modeling.
- **Community Grounding:** Whether organizing workshops, reviewing technical drafts, or advising teams on ethical deployment, my mission is to demystify applied AI so it becomes an accessible, accountable tool for practical empowerment.
